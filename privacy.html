Privacy Policy

Ardteist Prep · Last updated 1 November 2026

Ardteist Prep is a free Leaving Certificate practice app and website. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect personal data

There are no accounts, sign-ups or ads. We don't ask for your name, email address, age or location, and we don't use analytics or tracking.

Your progress stays on your device

Your answers, scores, bookmarks and CAO calculator entries are saved only in your browser's or the app's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or your browser data, or by uninstalling the app.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your device's IP address, as with any website request. See Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children and students

Ardteist Prep is made for secondary-school students. Because it collects no personal data, students of any age can use it without sharing information about themselves.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

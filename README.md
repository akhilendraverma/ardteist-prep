# ardteist-prep
Leaving Cert Prep

Ardteist Prep is published: a working Leaving Cert practice site covering all eight subjects you picked. “Ardteist” is the Irish name for the Leaving Cert.

What’s in it:

Questions: 110 exam-style questions across Maths, English, Gaeilge, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science and Applied Maths. They’re tagged by topic and level (Higher/Ordinary), and every one has a worked solution.
Two question types: multiple-choice questions mark themselves. Written questions show the solution, and the student rates themselves as “got it”, “partly” or “missed”.
Practice tools: students can filter by level, topic, “not tried”, “needs work” or bookmarks.
Timed mock tests: for one subject or mixed, with a score and a list of topics to revisit at the end.
My progress page: shows accuracy and weak topics per subject, plus bookmarks. Progress is saved in the student’s own browser.
CAO points calculator: counts the best six subjects and adds the 25 bonus points for Higher Maths.
Exam structure for each subject: shown on every subject card, for example “Oral 40%” for Irish and “70% exam in Python, 30% coursework” for Computer Science.
